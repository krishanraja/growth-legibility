# Legibility outreach template

Approved by Krish on 21 October 2026 as the reusable basis for Legibility outreach, following the clear pilot invitations prepared in Control Center. This advances the builder-to-agent acquisition loop. Control Center remains the source of truth for named prospects, evidence, drafts and approvals.

## Template

Subject: 30-day free beta trial of Legibility for [company]

Hi [first name],

I'm Krish, building Legibility. It gives AI shopping agents product information they can work with: send it a supported product-page URL or barcode, and it returns details such as the product name, price and stock availability, with confidence scores and timestamps.

For [company], it could sit alongside your existing product data to help cover [specific supported pages relevant to their workflow]. If it performs better on particular sites, it could also replace that part of your page-reading process. [Product name] would still handle the comparison and recommendation.

The aim is less work extracting product details and a clearer view of which data your agent can trust. [One short, source-checked example relevant to this prospect, if available.] The pilot would test coverage, accuracy and cost against what you use today.

Legibility is in pilot mode, and I'm looking for beta testers for a 30-day free trial in exchange for feedback. Would you be interested in trying it on a few of [company]'s [relevant supported] product URLs?

Krish
https://legibility.io

## Use

- Explain who Krish is and what Legibility does before discussing demo results.
- Tailor workflow fit to current company evidence. Do not assume their existing system is broken or that they need a replacement.
- Present reduced extraction work as the intended benefit; establish actual coverage, accuracy, cost and savings through the pilot. No unsupported performance promises.
- Keep the 30-day free trial explicit. This offer does not establish unlimited usage, automated expiry, billing terms or provisioned access.
- Use measured examples as supporting evidence. Distinguish merchant observations from listing snapshots, confidence scores from guarantees, and selected-variant stock from all-variant availability.
- Verify current product support, approved ICP and named-person context before preparing future outreach. This is a reusable structure, not permission for generic list sequences.
- Adapt to the contact channel: email subject for email; a copyable note for LinkedIn. Keep recipient-specific evidence and demos in Control Center, never this public repository.
- Keep drafts ready for Krish to send. Template approval does not authorise sending, scheduling or account provisioning.

## Variant: someone Krish has already reached for Mindmake

Approved by Krish on 27 October 2026, under his ruling the same day: "if I've already reached out to this person for MindMake, legibility is positioned as a partner product." The template above is unchanged and stays the opening for everyone else.

Use this variant only when all of these hold:

- Control Center's record for the person shows a Mindmake touch;
- no Mindmake item about them is still open;
- they are not a Mindmake client;
- the last Mindmake touch is at least 14 days old.

Replace the template's first sentence, "I'm Krish, building Legibility.", with the two sentences for that record. Everything after it stays word for word.

| What Control Center records | The two opening sentences |
|---|---|
| Krish's own Mindmake note went, with no reply | I wrote to you in [month] about Mindmake. This is a separate note about Legibility, one of the products we build at Mindmake. |
| They replied to Krish's Mindmake note | Thanks for your reply in [month] about Mindmake. This is a separate note about Legibility, one of the products we build at Mindmake. |
| Krish talked with them about Mindmake | Thanks for talking with me in [month] about Mindmake. This is a separate note about Legibility, one of the products we build at Mindmake. |
| Any other Mindmake touch: a note drafted but not shown as sent, or a Mindmake note a colleague sent for Krish (and any reply to it) | I'm Krish. Legibility is one of the products we build at Mindmake. |

### Use

- [month] is the month of the note, reply or conversation in Control Center's record. Add the year when it is not this year, as in "in September 2025". Never write "recently", "last week" or a date from memory.
- Visibility notes are not reaching out for Mindmake: podcast pitches, guest invites, press asks and the visibility notes a colleague sends for Krish do not count. Someone touched only by those gets the template's own opening above, "I'm Krish, building Legibility.", and Mindmake is not named.
- Mindmake appears only in those two sentences. No Mindmake link, offer, price, proof, client name or second ask. The one offer stays Legibility's 30-day free trial in exchange for feedback, and "free" stays beside it so it is never read as Mindmake's paid proof.
- Never write "partner", "partnership", "Mindmake-backed" or "in partnership with Mindmake". Legibility and Mindmake are one company. Write "Mindmake", never "Mindmaker".
- No "following up", "circling back", "as discussed" or "in case you missed it", and nothing about a missing reply. Never reuse what they told Krish about Mindmake, and never mention a Mindmake proposal or its terms.
- Send it as a new email under the template subject, never as a reply in the Mindmake thread. On LinkedIn the same two sentences open the copyable note. Never draft it in a colleague's mailbox or voice.
- Cite the person's Control Center record in the draft's sources.
- Write nothing in these cases, which the engine holds: a Mindmake item about them is still open, they are a Mindmake client, the last Mindmake touch is under 14 days old, or the record is unknown.
- An introduction request opens with the same two sentences, then makes its one introduction ask.
- Approving this variant does not authorise sending, scheduling or account provisioning.
